# Design System Document

This document outlines the core aesthetic principles and technical specifications of our design system. It serves as a single source of truth for designers and developers to ensure consistency and coherence across all products.

---

## 1. Color Palette

Our color palette is designed to be accessible, vibrant, and aligned with our brand identity.

### Color Mode
The current interface operates in a **dark** color mode, prioritizing readability and reducing eye strain in low-light environments.

### Brand Colors
These are the foundational colors defining our brand's presence:
*   **Primary Color:** `#BB86FC` - A vibrant purple that serves as our main accent and interactive element color.
*   **Neutral Color:** `#121212` - A deep, dark gray used for backgrounds, surfaces, and foundational elements.

---

## 2. Typography

Typography plays a crucial role in conveying information and establishing our brand's voice.

### Headline Font
Our headlines use **Space Grotesk** to create a modern and impactful presence.

### Body Font
For all body text, we use **Inter**, chosen for its excellent readability and versatility across various screen sizes.

### Label Font
Interactive elements and labels also utilize **Inter**, ensuring consistency and clarity in user interfaces.

---

## 3. Shape and Form

The geometry of our UI elements contributes significantly to the overall feel of the product.

### Roundedness
Our UI elements feature a **subtle roundedness (1)**, offering a modern and approachable aesthetic without sacrificing crispness.

---

## 4. Spacing

Consistent spacing is vital for creating visual hierarchy and a comfortable user experience.

### Spacing Scale
We utilize a **normal (2)** spacing scale, providing a balanced layout that is neither too dense nor excessively sparse, optimizing for both information density and visual comfort.
